What UPSC evaluators actually check

Your answer is read against the same things a GS or Optional paper is marked on.

Structure

A clear introduction that frames the question, a body organised by dimension (political, economic, social, and so on where relevant), and a conclusion that's forward-looking rather than repeated.

Most marks lost on otherwise-correct UPSC answers come from structure, not facts.

Value addition

Diagrams, keywords, quotes, committee references, and current examples that show reading beyond the standard material. We check whether your answer has any, and where you could add one.

A flagged, specific gap here is worth more than a general "add more examples" note.
