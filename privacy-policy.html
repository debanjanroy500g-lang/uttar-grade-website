Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Uttar Grade collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. We've tried to write it plainly rather than in dense legal language.

1. What we collect

  • Your Razorpay Payment ID and the amount paid, used to track your remaining credits.
  • The answer script you upload (a PDF or image), sent to our AI provider to generate your evaluation.
  • Basic payment details handled directly by Razorpay, such as your email or phone number if you provide them at checkout. We do not see or store your card or UPI details, Razorpay processes these independently under its own security standards.
  • The email address you give at Razorpay checkout, stored with your credits so we can find your purchase if you contact us.
  • A record of your consent (the date, and which version of the notice you agreed to) and a simple log of each evaluation (the exam chosen and which AI service handled it, never the content of your answer).
  • A small piece of data in your browser (your Access Code, kept in your browser's local storage) so you stay logged in on that device. It is sent only with your own requests, and logging out clears it.

2. What we don't do

  • We do not permanently store your uploaded answer scripts on our own servers after an evaluation is generated.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising scripts on this site.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Who we share data with

To provide the service, some information is shared with:

  • Razorpay, to process your payment securely.
  • Our AI providers: Google (Gemini) and Anthropic (Claude), which read and evaluate your uploaded script. Anthropic's terms for its API state that it does not train its models on data we send it. Google's free Gemini service is different: Google states that it may use submitted content to improve its products and that human reviewers may read it. For that reason we ask you not to put your name, roll number, phone number or other personal details on the pages you upload. Please see each provider's own privacy policy for how it handles data sent to its API.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the typefaces used on this site (your browser requests them directly from Google).
  • Supabase, our database and backend provider, which stores your Payment ID, the email you gave at checkout, and your credit balance.

We do not share your data with anyone beyond what's needed to run the service.

4. How long we keep data

Your Payment ID and credit balance are kept for as long as your account or credits remain active, so you can continue using your purchased credits. Uploaded scripts are not retained after processing. If you'd like your data deleted entirely, contact us and we will remove what we can, subject to records Razorpay may be legally required to retain for payment compliance.

5. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what data we hold about you, or to delete it. We'll respond within a reasonable time. Some information tied to a completed payment may need to be retained for accounting or legal purposes even after a deletion request. You can also withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. This stops any further processing, but we cannot recall content an AI provider has already processed.

6. Children's privacy

Uttar Grade is intended for adults preparing for competitive exams and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from people under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves, for example if we change AI providers or add new features. Updates will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please email us at uttargrade.support@gmail.com.
